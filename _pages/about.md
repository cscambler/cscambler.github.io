I am a postdoctoral research fellow in philosophy 
at All Souls College, Oxford University. My main 
areas of research interest are logic, metaphysics, 
and foundations of mathematics. My current research projects
focus on the significance of the independence problem in set theory, 
non-classical solutions to semantic paradoxes and metaphyiscal puzzles, 
and the question of the reducibility of mathematics to logic.
